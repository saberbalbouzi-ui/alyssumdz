FAQ

Questions we are asked most.

What does SABER DIGITAL PRO do?

We are a digital studio covering strategy, SEO content, website design and development, e-commerce optimization and AI-powered website assistants. We usually combine several of these into one programme.

Which languages do you work in?

English, French and Arabic. Each version is written and designed natively, including full right-to-left layouts for Arabic, rather than being a literal translation.

Do you work with existing websites?

Yes. We can audit, refresh and extend what you already have, or rebuild from scratch when that is the better choice. We will tell you honestly which makes sense.

How do you price projects?

Each project is scoped individually from your goals, languages and volume of work. After a first conversation we send a clear proposal with deliverables and timing.

How long does SEO take to show results?

It depends on competition, your current site and how consistently content is published. Organic growth builds over months, not days, and we do not promise rankings. We plan for steady, measurable improvement.

Can an AI assistant really help my business?

For repetitive questions and first-response handling, yes. We define what the assistant may answer, base it on your approved content, and hand serious enquiries to your team via WhatsApp or email.

Will you write the content or do I need to supply it?

We can do either. We commonly interview your team, research your market and write the content ourselves, then review it with you before publishing.

How do we get started?

Send us a short description of your business and goals through the form or on WhatsApp. We will reply to discuss scope and suggest the best first step.

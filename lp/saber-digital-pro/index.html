How content is researched and approved

Content that touches health, finance, law or technical facts is never published on our word alone.

  1. 01

    Research

    We read your material, interview your team and review the sources your market relies on, and we keep a source list.

  2. 02

    Fact-check

    Claims, figures and names are checked against those sources. Anything we cannot verify is removed or flagged for you.

  3. 03

    Approval

    Your subject-matter expert reviews the draft where accuracy matters, and nothing is published without your approval.

    A multi-specialty clinic wants patients to understand a service and ask with confidence.

    Patient FAQ structure

    1. Is this service right for me? Start with a consultation
    2. How should I prepare?
    3. What happens during the visit?
    4. Costs and insurance: who to ask
    5. How do I contact the clinic?

    Safety checklist

    • Clinical statements supplied or approved by the clinic's doctors
    • No promises of outcomes or cures
    • Emergency guidance and contact details are easy to find
    • Plain, reassuring language at an accessible reading level

    • Booking requests per service page
    • Visibility for treatment and symptom searches
    • Time from visit to first contact
  • Demonstration concept — not a client engagementE-commerce

    Category and product copy, plus a checkout clarity checklist

    An online store has solid traffic but thin category copy and hesitation at checkout.

    Category and product copy treatment

    1. Category intro: who it is for and how to choose
    2. Product title pattern: benefit, type, key specification
    3. Short description: three benefit lines
    4. Specifications table, accurate and consistent
    5. Delivery and returns line beside the add-to-cart button

    Checkout clarity checklist

    • Total cost, including delivery, visible before checkout
    • Delivery areas and timing stated honestly
    • Payment methods shown before the form
    • Returns policy linked from the cart
    • Guest checkout and clear error messages

    • Category-page organic visibility
    • Add-to-cart and checkout completion rates
    • Support questions about delivery and returns

07FAQ

Questions we are asked most.

Are the samples on this page real client projects?

No. They are demonstration concepts authored for this page and are labelled as such. We do not show client logos, quotes or results that we cannot document.

Which languages do you work in?

English, French and Arabic. Each version is written and designed natively, including full right-to-left layouts for Arabic, rather than being a literal translation.

Do you work with existing websites?

Yes. We can audit, refresh and extend what you already have, or rebuild from scratch when that is the better choice. We will tell you honestly which makes sense.

How do you price projects?

Each project is scoped individually from your goals, languages and volume of work. After a first conversation we send a written proposal with deliverables and the points where you review and approve.

How long does SEO take to show results?

It depends on competition, your current site and how consistently content is published. Organic growth builds over months, not days, and we do not promise rankings. We plan for steady, measurable improvement.

Can an AI assistant really help my business?

For repetitive questions and first-response handling, yes. We define what the assistant may answer, base it on your approved content, and hand serious enquiries to your team via WhatsApp or email.

Will you write the content or do I need to supply it?

We can do either. We commonly interview your team, research your market and write the content ourselves, then review it with you before publishing.

What happens when I press “Prepare my request”?

The page builds a message from your details on your own device. Nothing is sent. You then choose WhatsApp or email and press send yourself, or you copy the message.
